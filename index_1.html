    How we work

    The rules that never change.

    When to call

    Weekdays, 9 to 11 am or 2 to 4 pm Colorado time (Mountain Time). Use the TextNow line. Business numbers only.

    Golden rules

    • Smile while you talk. People can hear it.
    • Talk less, listen more.
    • Never argue. Someone who says no today can call back later.
    • Always send the preview link, even if they say no.
    • Technical questions go to Sophie.

    "Don't call me"

    If anyone asks you not to call again, mark them Do not call and never contact them again.
